1. Our Accessibility Commitment

Carewell Health Clinic believes that compassionate healthcare should be easily accessible to everyone. We are deeply committed to ensuring both physical and digital accessibility for all individuals, including persons with sensory, mobility, cognitive, and communicative impairments.

We continually audit and refine our practices to create an inclusive, barrier-free environment across our medical plazas and digital patient touchpoints.

2. Digital Web Standards (WCAG 2.1 Level AA)

Our website and online patient portal are built in conformance with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA specifications established by the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C). Key accessibility features include:

  • High Contrast & Typography: Carefully tested color contrast ratios exceeding 4.5:1 for text readability and clear typography scales.
  • Keyboard Navigation: Fully operational navigation using only keyboard tabs, enter, and arrow keys with visible focus rings.
  • Screen Reader Optimization: Semantic HTML5 structures, meaningful alternative text (`alt` tags) for images, and ARIA labels on all interactive controls.
  • Resizable Text: Scalable layout designs supporting up to 200% browser zoom without loss of functionality or horizontal scroll distortion.

3. Physical Clinic Facilities & ADA Accommodations

Our medical center is fully compliant with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) standards for accessible medical care facilities:

  • Dedicated accessible parking spaces located adjacent to zero-step automatic entryways.
  • Wide elevators, power-assisted doors, and unobstructed corridors for wheelchair and mobility aid navigation.
  • Adjustable examination tables and wheelchair-accessible diagnostic equipment.
  • Service animals welcome in all patient consultation and waiting rooms.
  • Sensory-sensitive early morning consultation options for neurodivergent individuals upon request.

4. Language Assistance & Interpreter Services

To eliminate communication barriers in clinical diagnosis, Carewell Clinic provides free auxiliary aids and language assistance services to individuals whose primary language is not English, or who are Deaf or hard of hearing:

Free Medical Translation & ASL Interpreters Over-the-phone and video remote interpreting (VRI) available in over 150 languages, including certified American Sign Language (ASL) interpreters. Please notify our scheduling desk when booking your appointment.
